About Ofrey

The right music changes how a moment is remembered.

Ofrey is a guitarist, bassist and composer with 26 years of experience on guitar. His career has been shaped by performance, composition and a genuine connection with music.

For events, his focus is elegant instrumental interpretations of well-known international classics, romantic favorites and songs from multiple generations—music that adds atmosphere without competing with the moment.
